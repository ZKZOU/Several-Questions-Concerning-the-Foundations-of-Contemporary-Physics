# Several Questions Concerning the Foundations of Contemporary Physics

1. A constrained set of phenomena composed of a finite number of phenomena can be fitted by countless mathematical models, and the mathematical models in textbooks are not unique. So why have the textbook models become the authoritative mainstream? The problem of underdetermination.

2. Has physics really avoided metaphysics? Is the physical-mathematical modeling that departs from 3D intuitive representation also a kind of metaphysics?

3. Are the rules of cosmic evolution at the quantum scale really time-symmetric? Is there any phenomenological evidence for this?

4. Contemporary physics is known for abandoning commitments to ontological pictures and turning instead to precise mathematical derivation, but is mathematics really rigorous? The causal law under the light-speed constraint in 3D space is the cornerstone of contemporary physical theories, but can the mathematical modeling of quantum theory built on abstract Hilbert space preserve neighborhood (adjacency) relations and map them back to 3D space, so as to ensure that this mathematics does not violate the causal law under the light-speed constraint at the level of mathematical logic?

5. Overall, is the narrative of contemporary physics characterized by double standards? When criticizing metaphysics, it invokes mathematical rigor to emphasize its own position, while when defending its own lack of mathematical rigor, it applies another set of standards.
